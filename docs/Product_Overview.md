# CarePulse AI – Product Overview

## 1. Product Overview

CarePulse AI is an AI-powered healthcare monitoring platform designed to help clinical teams monitor patients, identify potential health risks, prioritize alerts, and improve the efficiency of clinical workflows.

The platform combines patient monitoring data, risk scoring, alert management, and healthcare analytics into a centralized solution.

## 2. Problem Statement

Healthcare teams often manage large volumes of patient monitoring data and clinical alerts. Manual review and prioritization can make it difficult to quickly identify high-risk patients and may lead to delayed intervention.

CarePulse AI aims to help healthcare teams identify important patient signals earlier and prioritize clinical attention based on risk.

## 3. Target Users

### Clinicians
Need to quickly identify high-risk patients and review relevant patient information.

### Care Managers
Need visibility into patient health trends, alerts, interventions, and outcomes.

### Healthcare Administrators
Need operational insights and KPIs to monitor the effectiveness of healthcare services.

### Patients
Need continuous monitoring and timely intervention when abnormal health signals are detected.

## 4. Business Objectives

- Improve visibility into patient health trends.
- Prioritize high-risk patients and clinical alerts.
- Reduce manual effort involved in monitoring and reporting.
- Improve clinical response time.
- Provide actionable healthcare analytics.
- Support data-driven decision making.

## 5. Product Goals

- Provide a centralized patient monitoring experience.
- Identify abnormal patient trends.
- Prioritize alerts based on risk.
- Provide clinicians with actionable information.
- Track operational and clinical KPIs.
- Enable AI-assisted risk detection and alert prioritization.

## 6. MVP Scope

The Minimum Viable Product will include:

1. Patient Management
2. Vital Signs Monitoring
3. Patient Risk Scoring
4. Clinical Alert Management
5. Healthcare Analytics Dashboard

## 7. Key Features

### Patient Management
- Patient profile
- Patient monitoring status
- Risk level
- Patient history

### Vital Monitoring
- Blood pressure
- Heart rate
- Oxygen saturation (SpO2)
- Temperature
- Historical trends

### Risk Detection
- Patient risk score
- Risk classification
- Risk factors
- Abnormal trend detection

### Alert Management
- Alert creation
- Alert prioritization
- Alert assignment
- Alert acknowledgement
- Alert escalation
- Alert resolution

### Analytics
- Total monitored patients
- High-risk patients
- Open alerts
- Average response time
- Alert resolution rate
- Escalation rate

## 8. Success Metrics

The initial product success metrics will include:

- Reduction in average clinical alert response time
- Alert resolution rate
- High-risk patient identification rate
- Reduction in manual monitoring effort
- Clinician adoption rate
- Patient monitoring coverage

Future versions may include:

- Predictive patient risk models

- Automated clinical summaries
- Personalized patient risk predictions
- Integration with Electronic Health Records (EHR)
- FHIR/API integrations

- Advanced healthcare analytics

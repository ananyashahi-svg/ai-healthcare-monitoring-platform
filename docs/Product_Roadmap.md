# CarePulse AI – Product Roadmap

## 1. Roadmap Objective

The CarePulse AI roadmap defines the evolution of the product from an MVP healthcare monitoring platform into a scalable, analytics-driven and responsible AI-enabled clinical workflow solution.

The roadmap is organized around:

- Clinical workflow value
- Patient monitoring needs
- Operational efficiency
- Product adoption
- Data maturity
- Responsible AI
- Scalability

---

# 2. Product Vision

> Help healthcare teams identify patient risk earlier, prioritize clinical workflows, and improve operational visibility through reliable monitoring data and responsible AI.

---

# 3. Strategic Product Themes

| Theme | Objective |
|---|---|
| Patient Monitoring | Improve visibility into patient health signals |
| Risk Detection | Identify higher-risk patients efficiently |
| Alert Management | Reduce alert overload and improve prioritization |
| Clinical Workflow | Improve acknowledgement, escalation and resolution |
| Analytics | Enable data-driven operational decisions |
| AI Assistance | Reduce manual information-review effort |
| Scalability | Prepare the platform for larger healthcare programs |
